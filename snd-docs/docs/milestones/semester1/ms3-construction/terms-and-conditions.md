## Conditions of Use

SND is intended for authorised users to create, configure, deploy and manage virtualised 5G networks for research and experimentation, within the infrastructure provided by Instituto de Telecomunicações (IT).

- Users must review and explicitly approve the proposed network configuration before it is deployed. Any corrective changes the system proposes after a failed deployment must also be approved before they run.
- Users can access and manage only their own networks and configurations. Administrative operations, such as updating the system's technical knowledge base, are restricted to authorised administrators.
- The following are prohibited: unauthorised access, attempts to bypass system restrictions, interfering with other users' deployments, and using the platform outside its intended research environment.

These conditions ensure the platform is used responsibly, protect the shared infrastructure, and prevent unauthorised operations or interference between users.

## Liability Disclaimers

- SND relies on AI-assisted planning and automated deployment, which can produce incorrect configurations or failed deployments. The system includes validation and error handling, but successful deployment and operation cannot be guaranteed in every scenario.
- SND is designed for research and experimentation in laboratory environments, not for production telecommunications networks. Its reliability and suitability for critical or commercial use are not guaranteed.

These limitations set realistic expectations about what the system can do and how it can fail, without excluding any responsibilities required by applicable law.

SND is intended for research and experimentation within IT, and no paid services or subscriptions are currently planned, so commercial cancellation and refund policies do not apply.

Users can stop using the platform at any time and request the deletion of their account and associated personal data, subject to data protection requirements and retention obligations (see [GDPR](./rgpd.md)).

Any legal disputes arising from the use of SND are handled according to applicable law and the relevant institutional policies, with jurisdiction determined by the applicable legal rules.

This gives the platform a clear legal framework and makes it clear how potential legal conflicts are resolved.
