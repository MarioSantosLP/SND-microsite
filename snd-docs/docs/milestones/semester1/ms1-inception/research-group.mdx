# Research Group

<LogoPanel src="/img/partners/atnog.png" alt="ATNoG logo" />

This project is conducted within the scope of **ATNoG** (Aveiro Telecommunications and Networking Group), a research group focused on innovation in communication networks, software, and related technologies.

ATNoG works across areas such as 5G and 6G networks, cloud computing, virtualization, software-defined networking, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, machine learning, IoT, and embedded systems. The group combines academic research with strong industry collaboration, working with companies such as Altice, Bosch, Nokia, NEC, and Huawei, while also participating in national and international research projects.

Its work has contributed to standardization organizations such as IEEE and IETF, as well as to several open-source initiatives, with networking and software serving as the foundation of its research activities.
