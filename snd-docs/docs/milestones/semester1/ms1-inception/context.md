Telecommunication networks have been evolving from traditional models, characterised by a high level of pre-planning and manual configuration, towards increasingly dynamic and programmable infrastructures. In this evolution, softwarisation, where network functions run as software rather than on dedicated hardware, plays an increasingly important role in the design, deployment and operation of networks.

At the same time, today's digital services rely more and more on the combined use of communication and computing resources. Although these two kinds of systems are distinct, they need to be coordinated and orchestrated together to meet the needs of services and their users.

This evolution leads to physical and virtual ICT infrastructures that are increasingly dynamic and must adapt to different usage scenarios. Configuring them by hand becomes harder and more time-consuming as they grow, which is the gap SND aims to address by automating the deployment of 5G core networks.
